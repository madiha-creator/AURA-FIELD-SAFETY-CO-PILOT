# Procedure: proc_generator_check
Title: Diesel Backup Generator Operational Check

## Step 1
Verify fuel oil level and battery terminal connections. Inspect air intake filter for blockages or debris.

## Step 2
Initiate no-load test start sequence. Monitor engine oil pressure gauge and confirm reading stabilizes between 25.0 and 65.0 PSI.

## Step 3
Attach vibration sensor to primary housing mount. Measure vibration frequency and verify readings remain between 10.0 and 60.0 Hz.

## Step 4
Allow generator to run for 10 minutes. Check exhaust system for thermal leaks before completing procedure logging.
