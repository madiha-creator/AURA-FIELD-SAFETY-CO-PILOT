# Procedure: proc_hydraulic_pump
Title: Main Hydraulic Pump Inspection and Pressure Calibration

## Step 1
Perform visual inspection of hydraulic pump housing, suction line, and high-pressure discharge fittings. Check fluid level in main reservoir.

## Step 2
Start hydraulic drive motor in low-idle mode. Verify main loop pressure gauge stabilizes within safe range of 1000.0 to 3000.0 PSI.

## Step 3
Check hydraulic reservoir fluid temperature gauge. Ensure operating oil temperature stays within 100.0 to 160.0 F under load.

## Step 4
Inspect return line filter indicator. Shut down drive motor and log pump maintenance entry if differential pressure exceeds limits.
