# Procedure: proc_coolant_flush
Title: Secondary Coolant Loop Flushing and Inspection

## Step 1
Inspect secondary coolant reservoir line connections for leaks or physical damage. Verify that isolation valves V-101 and V-102 are fully closed before starting fluid displacement.

## Step 2
Attach drain hose to low-point purge valve. Open purge valve slowly and verify coolant line pressure reads between 4.0 and 10.0 PSI during initial flow.

## Step 3
Monitor operational fluid temperature gauge. Confirm coolant temperature remains between 50.0 and 180.0 F throughout the flushing cycle.

## Step 4
Close purge valve, remove drain hose, and re-torque secondary line couplings to 35 ft-lbs. Record final pressure reading.
